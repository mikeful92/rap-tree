    How it works

    01

    Collect

    About 60 popular songs per artist from Genius. Guest verses and repeated hooks are stripped, so every line is the artist's own.

    02

    Compress

    Every artist gets the same amount of text. Append a chunk of one artist's lyrics to another's and count the extra bytes it costs to compress (Benedetto, Caglioti & Loreto, 2002).

    03

    Grow

    Neighbor-joining turns the distances into a tree, rebuilt from different song samples to show which branches hold up.
